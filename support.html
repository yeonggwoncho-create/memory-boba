Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I play Memory Boba?
    Watch the grid as a pattern of tapioca pearls flashes briefly. Memorize the pattern and tap all the highlighted pearls back from memory. As you clear levels, the grid expands from 3x3 to 5x5!
  • What are the game modes?
    • Classic Memory: Level up through scaling grids with 3 strikes.
    • Casual Memory: Relaxed, untimed practice with unlimited lives and undo.
    • Boba Memory: Memorize boba pearl placements with randomized variations.
    • Speed Classic: Fast-paced flashes and quick timer challenges.
    • Speed Custom: Customize grid dimensions, pearl count, and flash duration to test your brain limits!
  • Are there any in-app purchases or subscriptions?
    Memory Boba is free and designed to be a clean, fun brain training game for players of all ages.
  • Can I play offline?
    Yes! Memory Boba works completely offline without requiring an active internet connection.
