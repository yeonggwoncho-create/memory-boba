Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do step rewards work?
    Every 500 steps recorded in Apple Health unlocks 1 tapioca pearl (up to 10,000 steps/day). Tap pearls on the Home tab to claim coins!
  • When can I cash out?
    You can redeem your earned coins for USD via PayPal once your account has been active for 7 days. Requests are manually reviewed and processed within 5 business days.
  • Is my health data private?
    Yes, 100%. Boba Walk only reads activity data locally on your device to calculate rewards. We never sell, track, or share your health data with advertisers.
