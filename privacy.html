Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. What We Collect

  • Health Data (Read-only, with permission): Steps, walking distance, active energy, heart rate, and resting heart rate from Apple Health.
  • Reward Data: Coin balance, check-in history, and cash-out requests.
  • PayPal Email: Only when you voluntarily request a cash-out payout.
  • Anonymous Install ID: Used strictly to prevent multiple accounts or fraudulent activity.

2. How Health Data is Used

Your Health data is used exclusively to display your physical activity progress and calculate step rewards inside Boba Walk.

  • Health data is processed locally on your device.
  • Health data is NEVER used for advertising or marketing.
  • Health data is NEVER sold or shared with third-party brokers.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to serve non-personalized ads. We do not track you across third-party apps or websites.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete all application data at any time inside the app by going to Settings > Delete my data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@bobawalk.app.
