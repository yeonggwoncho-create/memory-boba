Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Overview

Memory Boba is built with user privacy as our top priority. We believe in providing an enjoyable brain puzzle experience without collecting unnecessary personal information.

2. Information Collection & Use

Memory Boba does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information:

  • No user accounts, names, or email registrations are required to play.
  • High scores are stored strictly locally on your device (via local storage) and are never sent to external servers.
  • We do not track your location or access your contacts, camera, or health data.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

Memory Boba does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or data brokers. Your gameplay stays completely private on your device.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because Memory Boba does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and is safe for users of all ages.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at anthonycho3014@gmail.com.
